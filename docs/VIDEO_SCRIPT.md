# 3-minute demo video: script

Record at 1280 px or wider, light theme, the deployed site (or `make demo`). Narration in quotes.

| Time | Screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing page, live stats | "Bangladesh ships most of its garments and medicines through one port, Chattogram. When it slows, orders miss their dates. ShipperFast shows that delay before it reaches your orders." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Log in → Control Tower, real data: KPI strip, daily brief, live port map | "This is today, from public data: Chittagong Port Authority's own schedule and berth reports, polled every fifteen minutes. Sixteen ships waiting, a median wait of about three hours, and the brief says why, with a source on every number." |
| 0:40–1:00 | Ports & Vessels: map, yard bars, gate chart, a ship's hover card | "Every ship at the berths and the anchorage, how full the yards are, and how many containers crossed the gates. A waiting ship's dock time comes from three years of real berthings." |
| 1:00–1:20 | Data selector → "Cyclone shuts Chattogram + denim mill flood" | "Now a what-if. A synthetic world, fictional companies, clearly badged: a cyclone closes the port and a denim mill floods." |
| 1:20–1:45 | Disruption Radar: the cyclone event, fused confidence, corroboration | "News and the hazard feed agree, so confidence rises to eighty-six percent. Every claim links to its evidence." |
| 1:45–2:05 | Supplier Network: Chattogram and the mill as single points of failure | "The supplier graph shows what breaks: these orders depend on that one mill, and every export crosses Chattogram." |
| 2:05–2:25 | Recommendation drawer: export via Mongla, accept | "Nexus proposes costed actions: export through Mongla, worth about eighty-nine thousand dollars, at ninety-five percent confidence. One click to accept." |
| 2:25–2:45 | Scenario Studio: with Nexus vs without | "Same shock, with and without the actions: fewer late orders, higher on-time delivery, revenue at risk cut by a third." |
| 2:45–3:00 | Back to the landing page | "Open data, honest models, decisions with evidence. ShipperFast: see port delays before they reach your orders." |

Numbers change with live data: read them off the screen when recording.
